Paraguay offers one of the most open and fastest residency policies in the world. Below we detail the definitive steps under the current immigration law.

## Main Requirements

* Criminal record certificate from your country of origin, duly legalized or apostilled.
* Apostilled birth certificate.
* Marital status or apostilled marriage certificates (if applicable).
* Copy of your passport legalized by a notary (escribano) in Paraguay.

Once the documents are submitted to the Dirección General de Migraciones (General Directorate of Migration), the temporary residency card (valid for 2 years) is issued within 60 to 90 days, allowing you to apply for your Cédula de Identidad Civil (civil ID card).
