# How Much Does It Cost to Live in Paraguay? A Real Price Analysis for 2026

If you are looking for a country with a low tax burden, a warm climate and an affordable cost of living, Paraguay is probably on your radar. But what is the real cost of living here today? 

Many online databases accumulate outdated data that does not reflect recent inflation or the reality of the best neighborhoods in Asunción. In this guide we break down real monthly costs based on surveys updated as of **June 2026**.

---

## 1. Rent and Housing

Rent will undoubtedly be your biggest recurring expense. Prices vary dramatically depending on the city and, above all, the neighborhood.

In **Asunción**, the areas preferred by foreigners, expats and digital nomads are **Villa Morra, Las Mercedes, Mburucuyá, Manora and Ycua Satí** (the corporate district). 

Here is a typical breakdown of rents:
* **Modern studio apartment in a building with amenities (pool, gym, doorman):** 400 - 700 USD/month.
* **2-bedroom apartment in Villa Morra:** 700 - 1,200 USD/month.
* **3-bedroom house with yard and pool in Greater Asunción (Luque or Lambaré):** 600 - 1,000 USD/month.

## 2. Food and Grocery Basket

Paraguay is one of the world's largest exporters of beef and soy, which translates into very competitive prices for fresh food.

A standard monthly shop at chain supermarkets such as **Stock, Superseis or Delimarket** ranges from **200 to 300 USD per person**.

### Prices of staple products at the supermarket:
* **Prime Beef (1 kg):** 45,000 PYG (~6.10 USD)
* **Chicken Breast (1 kg):** 28,000 PYG (~3.80 USD)
* **Whole Milk (1 liter):** 6,500 PYG (~0.88 USD)
* **Dozen Eggs:** 12,000 PYG (~1.60 USD)
* **White Rice (1 kg):** 7,000 PYG (~0.95 USD)
* **Bottled Water (1.5 liters):** 4,000 PYG (~0.54 USD)

Eating out is also affordable. The executive lunch ("menú del día", or set menu) at restaurants in Asunción runs around **25,000 to 35,000 PYG** (3.40 - 4.80 USD). A dinner for two at a good restaurant in Villa Morra with wine and dessert costs approximately **180,000 to 250,000 PYG** (24.50 - 34.00 USD).

## 3. Utilities (Electricity, Internet, Mobile)

Public utilities and telecommunications in Paraguay are stable and inexpensive compared with European or North American standards.

* **Electricity (ANDE):** It is the cheapest rate in the region. However, because of the summer heat, the air conditioning will be on almost 24/7. An average bill for a 2-room apartment runs around **350,000 to 500,000 PYG** (48 - 68 USD) in the hot months. In winter, it can drop below 20 USD.
* **Fiber-Optic Internet (Tigo / Personal / Claro):** A stable residential plan of unlimited 100 Mbps costs about **130,000 PYG** (17.80 USD) per month.
* **Mobile Phone:** A prepaid or postpaid plan with plenty of data costs about **90,000 PYG** (12.30 USD) per month.

---

## 4. Transportation and Mobility

Traditional public transport consists of colectivos (city buses). The conventional fare costs **2,300 PYG** (0.31 USD) and the differentiated fare (with air conditioning) costs **3,400 PYG** (0.46 USD).

However, most foreigners opt for ride-hailing apps such as **Bolt and Uber**, which are extremely cheap and safe in Paraguay. A typical 5-kilometer ride in Asunción costs around **18,000 to 25,000 PYG** (2.40 - 3.40 USD).

If you prefer to drive your own car:
* **Liter of Nafta (Gasoline), 95 octane:** 7,800 PYG (~1.06 USD).
