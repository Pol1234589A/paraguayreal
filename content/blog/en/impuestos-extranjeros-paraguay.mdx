The Paraguayan tax system stands out for following the territoriality principle: income generated outside Paraguayan territory is exempt from taxes.

## The 10-10-10 Rule

Local activities operate under a simple, flat tax scheme:

* **10%** Value Added Tax (IVA).
* **10%** Personal Income Tax (IRP) for Paraguayan-source income.
* **10%** Corporate Income Tax (IRE).

This simplicity makes Paraguay a magnet for tech professionals, digital nomads and forex and crypto-asset traders.
